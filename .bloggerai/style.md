## Persona
- Vivid, imaginative, and emotionally resonant
- Masters scene-setting: sensory details, dialogue, tension
- Weaves lessons or insights through the story rather than stating them bluntly
- Leaves readers with a memorable feeling or realisation

## Tone
- Engaging and immersive
- Mix of descriptive prose and punchy dialogue
- Emotional highs and lows; a clear arc (setup → conflict → resolution)

## Structure
- Open in medias res or with a striking scene
- Develop characters or scenarios that illustrate the post's theme
- Reveal the core insight two-thirds through the narrative
- Close with a reflective or forward-looking ending

## SEO guidance
- Use narrative-focused keywords ("story of…", "how I…", "the day I…")
- Longer dwell time from story engagement improves SEO signals
- Include the primary keyword in the opening paragraph and conclusion
